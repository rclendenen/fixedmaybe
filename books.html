Unspoken Goodbyes

A Daughter's Story of Grief, Love, and Healing

Elizabeth Green is a devoted wife, mother, "Gammy," and a storyteller at heart. She carries both tenderness and tenacity in everything she does. Her father was her hero, her protector, and her greatest cheerleader.

When he passed, the grief that followed became the foundation for this memoir. Unspoken Goodbyes: A Daughter's Story of Grief, Love, and Healing was born from heartbreak but rooted in hope.

Through these pages, Elizabeth invites readers into her journey of grieving, remembering, growing, and ultimately rediscovering joy. Surrounded by meaningful friendships, heartfelt conversations, and the love of her ever-expanding family, she has learned to live with a deep sense of purpose.

She believes in laughing often, loving boldly, and writing bravely. In honoring her father—who, like a mighty oak, stood tall in love and grace—Elizabeth shares her story with the hope that it helps you feel seen in your own.

Tony Boy Adventures

Tony Boy Adventures is a heartfelt children's book inspired by the stories I was told throughout my own childhood—tales that shaped my imagination, taught me valuable lessons, and stayed close to my heart. These were the kinds of stories that made me dream a little bigger and feel a little braver, and now they're becoming something I can share with the world.

This book brings those cherished memories to life through playful adventures, warm characters, and moments of wonder that capture the magic I felt growing up. Each story is written with the same love, joy, and comfort that filled the bedtime tales shared with me.

I can't wait to share Tony Boy Adventures with young readers and families everywhere in 2026, and I hope it becomes a book that creates treasured memories for your home just as those childhood stories did for me.

ProdaGal – From a Mother's Point of View

Elizabeth is currently working on her next book, ProdaGal – From a Mother's Point of View, a unique Bible study inspired by the parable of the Prodigal Son (Luke 15:11–32).

While Scripture highlights the father and his two sons, Elizabeth invites readers to step into the untold role of the mother—imagining her prayers, fears, and hopes as she watched her child drift away and ultimately return home.

This heartfelt and reflective study offers encouragement, biblical insight, and a tender perspective for parents navigating their own seasons of waiting, heartache, and hope.
