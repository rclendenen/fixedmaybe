Speaking Engagements

Elizabeth shares her story of grief, healing, and hope through powerful speaking engagements. Available for book clubs, women's groups, grief support events, and faith-based gatherings.

Speaking Topics

Elizabeth is available for book clubs, speaking engagements, special events, and community-focused programs. She also works closely with nursing homes, hospice care teams, caregivers, and families, offering compassionate conversations that bring comfort, understanding, and hope.

  • Compassionate Conversations for Nursing Homes & Hospice Communities
  • Supporting Caregivers & Families
  • Grief and Healing
  • Faith and Family
  • Finding Hope in Loss
